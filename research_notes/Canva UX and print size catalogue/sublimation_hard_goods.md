# Sublimation print/template sizes for hard goods (non-textile)

Research date: 2026-10-27. Conversion: 1 in = 25.4 mm. Paper references: A4 = 210 x 297 mm (8.27 x 11.69 in); A3 = 297 x 420 mm; US Letter = 215.9 x 279.4 mm; US Legal = 215.9 x 355.6 mm.

Source-quality warning for the report writer: search results for "X oz template size" are dominated by scraped/spam template pages (e.g. msoid.icmbio.gov.br, my-test.valpo.edu, live.museum.manchester.ac.uk, chw.dhis2.org, app.sterlingedmonton.com). These were NOT used as primary evidence; where they are the only source, the figure is flagged "low confidence". Two primary template hubs (swingdesign.com Unisub template pages) returned HTTP 429 and could not be read. Blank-supplier pages (JPP, Unisub resellers, Atlanta Vinyl, Signdepot) mostly list blank dimensions and link to downloadable template ZIPs rather than stating print areas in text.

## Q1. Template dimensions per product (full print area incl. wrap/bleed, and visible area)

### Takeaway
Only a subset of products has well-sourced numbers: 11oz/15oz mugs (~8.5 x 3.5 in / ~9 x 4 in), 20oz skinny tumbler (~9.3 x 8.2-8.3 in), 16oz glass can (9.4-9.75 x 4.2-5 in), 40oz handled tumbler (two-part wrap, top ~12.6 x 5.8 in), mouse pad 9.25 x 7.75 in, Unisub license plate 5.88 x 11.88 in, coasters 3.54-4 in, ChromaLuxe panels (nominal + 1/8 in bleed), puzzles A4 290 x 200 mm / A5 202 x 146 mm, pet tags ~1.25 in. Many requested items (espresso, enamel, kids mugs, wine tumblers, koozies, bottles, phone cases, keychains, clocks, cutting boards, bookmarks, door hangers, CR80 cards, tiles, magnets) had no reliable source found in this session; see Gaps.

### Inferences
- Mug wrap width (~8.5-9 in) is slightly less than mug circumference so the design does not run under the handle; 11oz and 15oz wraps both fit A4 landscape but NOT A4 portrait (215.9 mm and 228.6 mm > 210 mm).
- Gearment's accent-mug pixel sizes at 300 dpi (assumed) equal ~9.06 x 3.71 in full and 8.0 x 3.25 in bordered - consistent with the 8.5-9 in x 3.5-3.7 in range from sellers.
- The 20oz skinny wrap (8.2-8.3 in tall = 208-211 mm) is right at A4's 210 mm short edge, so it is effectively a US Letter/Legal product; on A4 it needs borderless printing or slight down-scaling. A3 is a safe choice.
- The 40oz handled tumbler top wrap (12.6-12.8 in = 320-325 mm) is the clearest case requiring larger-than-A4 media (US Legal 8.5 x 14 in or A3).
- The Unisub plate (301.6 mm long) is 4.6 mm longer than A4; it needs A3 (or Legal). A US 6 x 12 in plate (152.4 x 304.8 mm, unsourced nominal) would likewise exceed A4.
- ChromaLuxe 8x10 with bleed (206.4 mm wide) fits A4 portrait with ~1.8 mm per side; anything 8x12 in or larger would need A3.
- Small items (coasters, ornaments, pet tags, car coasters, keychains) are typically ganged several-up on one A4 sheet.

- Unisub template hub pages ([swingdesign.com templates](https://www.swingdesign.com/pages/unisub-sublimation-blank-templates)) were rate-limited (HTTP 429); they are the most likely single source for the missing Unisub items and should be checked directly.

## Q2. Recommended bleed and mirroring

### Takeaway
ChromaLuxe specifies at least 1/8 in bleed; tumbler makers add ~0.25 in seam overlap and print borderless/zero margin. Mirroring is required for standard sublimation paper transfers (explicitly in Sawgrass/Jennifer Maker workflows).

### Cited Findings
- ChromaLuxe artwork "at least a 1/8 in bleed"; 8x10 -> 8.125 x 10.125 in (i.e. 1/16 in per side) — [JPP ChromaLuxe](https://www.jpplus.com/chromaluxe-aluminum-photo-panels)
- Tumbler: set print margins to 0, enable mirror, trim white paper so ink runs to edges, ~0.25 in overlap at seam — [Jennifer Maker](https://jennifermaker.com/how-to-sublimate-a-40-oz-tumbler/)
- Mirror the design when using a Sawgrass printer — [Silhouette School blog](https://www.silhouetteschoolblog.com/2022/07/how-to-re-size-tumbler-wrap-templates.html)
- Template sellers recommend slightly oversized files because skinny tumbler brands vary — [kindtumbler Gumroad](https://kindtumbler.gumroad.com/l/cuxcw)
- Gearment accent mug: full template runs edge-to-edge; a smaller "bordered" size leaves white top/bottom (a built-in safe area) — [Gearment](https://help.gearment.com/en-us/article/mug-products-vsa3nr)

### Inferences
- A sensible default for a template catalogue: flat goods = nominal size + 1/16 to 1/8 in (1.5-3 mm) per side bleed; wraps = add ~0.25 in (6 mm) to circumference for overlap and extra height for trimming; always mirror for paper-transfer sublimation (not for POD uploads, where the provider handles it).

### Gaps
- No supplier-wide bleed standard found for coasters, ornaments, puzzles or plates (Unisub templates not readable). Visible vs full area for tapered tumblers is only described qualitatively.

## Q3. Which products exceed A4 and need A3/Legal/roll

### Takeaway
From sourced numbers: 40oz handled tumbler top wrap and the Unisub 5.88 x 11.88 in license plate exceed A4 and need Legal or A3; the 20oz skinny wrap is borderline (fits only borderless, 9.3 x 8.3 in variant does not fit); ChromaLuxe 355 x 280 mm panels need A3. Mugs (11/15oz), 16oz glass cans, mouse pads, coasters, A4/A5 puzzles, ChromaLuxe 5x7/8x10, ornaments and tags fit A4.

### Cited Findings
- 40oz wrap printed on 8.5 x 14 in Legal paper — [Jennifer Maker](https://jennifermaker.com/how-to-sublimate-a-40-oz-tumbler/); also Legal size used by [The Country Chic Cottage](https://www.thecountrychiccottage.net/sublimation/)
- License plate 149.2 x 301.6 mm — [Swing Design](https://swingdesign.com/products/unisub-sublimation-license-plate-blanks)
- 20oz skinny 9.3 x 8.2-8.3 in — [sublimationwrap Gumroad](https://sublimationwrap.gumroad.com/l/MemorialPictureMemorialsublimationdesigns20ozSkinnyTumbler), [rapidfiredesigns Gumroad](https://rapidfiredesigns.gumroad.com/l/nzfpu)

### Inferences
- A4 fit summary (computed): 11oz 215.9 x 88.9 mm -> A4 landscape; 15oz 228.6 x 101.6 -> A4 landscape; 16oz can 243.8 x 106.7 -> A4 landscape; 20oz skinny 236.2 x 208.3 -> A4 landscape (1.7 mm spare, borderless) / 236.2 x 210.8 -> does not fit A4; 40oz top 320.8 x 147.6 -> A3 or Legal; mouse pad 235 x 196.9 -> A4 either orientation; plate 149.2 x 301.6 -> A3 or Legal; ChromaLuxe 8x10+bleed 206.4 x 257.2 -> A4 portrait; puzzle A4 290 x 200 -> A4 with minimal bleed.
- 30oz tumblers are likely beyond A4 height if ~8-9 in tall with ~9.3-9.7 in circumference wrap, but this is unverified.

### Gaps
- No sourced dimensions for 30oz skinny, water bottles, sports bottles, wall clocks, cutting boards, large MDF signs or XL gaming mouse pads, which are the other likely A3/roll candidates.
