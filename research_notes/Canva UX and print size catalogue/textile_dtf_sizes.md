# Textile print sizes: DTF transfers, sublimation, and DTF gang sheets

Research date: 2026-10-27. Sources are mostly 2025-2026 vendor and print-shop guides. Primary pages were read in full (via raw HTML download) for Ninja Transfers, Kingdom DTF, xTool, ScreenPrinting.com (Ryonet), Transfer Kingdom, Rush Order Tees, Gearment, Printful Help Center, Gelato, Atlanta Vinyl, DTF Dallas, Transfer Superstars, t-shirt.ca, DTFSheet and Longforte (Sawgrass paper). Other items come from search-result snippets and are marked "(snippet)" where the full page was not read.

Unit conversions are arithmetic (1 in = 25.4 mm), rounded to the nearest mm. Sheet references: A4 = 210 × 297 mm (8.27 × 11.69 in); A3 = 297 × 420 mm (11.69 × 16.54 in); A3+ / Super A3 = 13 × 19 in (330 × 483 mm); DTF roll gang sheets are 22 in (559 mm) wide by default.

"Fits" column rule used throughout (my inference, not from a source): the design's bounding box must fit inside the sheet in either orientation with at least ~3 mm clearance per side for a desktop printer's unprintable margin. Sizes within 0–5 mm of the sheet edge are flagged "tight".

## 1. Apparel placements (DTF and sublimation)

### Takeaway
Vendors converge on: left chest ~3.5 × 2 in (range 2.5–4.5 in), centre chest ~8 × 4 in, full front ~11 × 11 in (10–12 in wide), full back ~12 × 14 in, oversize 13 × 15 in up to 15 × 18 in, upper back ~12 × 4 in, back-neck 2 × 2 to 3 × 3 in, short sleeve ~2.5 × 1 to 3 × 3 in, long-sleeve vertical ~2–2.5 × 12–18 in. Everything at or below centre chest fits on A4; an 11-in full front fits A3; anything 12 in wide or more needs A3+ (13 × 19 in) or a 22-in roll gang sheet.

### Cited Findings
- Ninja Transfers gives size range, average adult and youth size, and placement distance for 8 locations (left chest, centre chest, full front, sleeve, collar, upper back, full back, oversize); full front can go "up to 14 in" wide if the design is short. — [Ninja Transfers](https://ninjatransfers.com/pages/dtf-transfer-placement-guide)
- Ryonet/ScreenPrinting.com (23 Jan 2026) quick table: left chest 3.5 × 2; centre chest 8.5 × 4; full front 11 × 11 (up to 12 W); short sleeve 2.5 × 1; long sleeve 1–2 tall × up to 14 long; back collar 2.5 × 2.5 or 3 × 3; upper back 12 × 4; full back 12 × 14 (up to 13 × 16); neck label 2 × 2 or 3 × 3. — [ScreenPrinting.com](https://www.screenprinting.com/blogs/news/dtf-transfer-placement-guide)
- xTool notes the back-collar print "replaces the neck label" and long-sleeve linear prints are 2 W × 14–18 H. — [xTool](https://www.xtool.com/blogs/xtool-academy/dtf-transfer-size-chart)
- Printful's standard DTG front area is 15 × 18 in on three tee models in larger sizes only; smaller product sizes don't support it; legacy was 12 × 16 in. — [Printful](https://help.printful.com/hc/en-us/articles/18532732956828)
- Sleeve guidance conflicts: Ninja 2.5 × 1 in average vs xTool 3–4 W × 2–3.5 H vs Transfer Kingdom 2 × 12 in (which is a long-sleeve figure). — sources above.

### Inferences
- For a Canva-style preset catalogue, sensible default artboards are: Left chest 3.5 × 3.5 in, Centre chest 8 × 4 in, Full front 11 × 11 in (or 11 × 14 tall), Full back 12 × 14 in, Oversize 13 × 15 in, Upper back 12 × 4 in, Neck 3 × 3 in, Sleeve 3 × 3 in, Long sleeve 2.5 × 14 in, Youth front 9 × 9 in, Toddler 7 × 7 in, Infant 5 × 5 in.
- A4 covers every placement up to centre chest, all toddler/infant fronts and youth XS; A3 is needed for standard adult full front (≤11.5 in W) and upper back; anything ≥12 in wide (full back, oversize, 2XL+ fronts) exceeds A3's 297 mm width and requires A3+ (330 mm) or a 22-in roll.

### Gaps
- Hood print size: no source found.
- Crop top specific sizes: no source found (closest proxy is tank/centre chest or youth sizes).
- Bella+Canvas and Gildan official print area guides and Stahls' size charts were not retrieved (rate limiting); Printful was only available for the 15 × 18 placement.

## 3. DTF gang sheets: widths, lengths, spacing, file requirements

### Takeaway
22 in is the de facto commercial width (some vendors 22.4 in or 24 in; 60 cm is the metric equivalent; desktop/A3+ printers use 13 in / 33 cm rolls or A3/A3+ cut sheets). Standard lengths run from 22 × 12 to 22 × 240 in. Files: transparent PNG, 300 DPI at final size, not mirrored, 1/4–1/2 in between designs, 0.25–0.5 in from sheet edge, lines at least ~0.5 mm.

### Cited Findings
**Widths / formats**
- Film width is set by printer and roll, "most commonly 22 inches or 60 centimeters"; common commercial sizes 22 × 24, 22 × 36, 22 × 60, 22 × 120 in. — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/connect/article/dtf-gang-sheet-batch-production-guide) (4 Aug 2026)
- Atlanta Vinyl sells 22 in wide sheets at lengths 12, 24, 36, 48, 60, 72, 84, 96, 108, 120, 144, 180, 240 in; calls 22 × 12 "standard small", 22 × 24 "popular for mixed designs". Notes Canva reduces resolution if the sheet exceeds 83 in, and Silhouette Studio can't create proper sheets larger than 22 × 36. — [Atlanta Vinyl product](https://atlantavinylstore.com/products/custom-dtf-transfer-gang-sheet-upload); [Atlanta Vinyl blog](https://atlantavinylstore.com/blogs/news/dtf-gang-sheets-explained-how-to-maximize-every-inch) (16 Mar 2026)
- DTF Dallas: 22 × 240 in (20 ft) is the "highest-output format" and common upper limit for a single job; at 300 DPI it is 6600 × 72000 px; Canva/Word/Publisher cannot build it; 22 × 60 and 22 × 120 also common. — [DTF Dallas](https://dtfdallas.com/blogs/news/building-22x240-gang-sheet-best-software) (updated 25 Oct 2026)
- Transfer Superstars prints from 1 × 1 in up to 22.4 × 100 in. — [Transfer Superstars](https://www.transfersuperstars.com/blogs/news/dtf-file-requirements-how-to-prep-your-artwork-for-perfect-prints) (16 Jun 2026)
- Transfer Kingdom's largest sheet: up to 22 × 60 in. — [Transfer Kingdom](https://transferkingdom.com/blogs/articles/ultimate-dtf-transfer-size-chart-for-t-shirts-sweatshirts-hoodies)
- A 24 in wide gang sheet is offered by DTF Transfers Now (snippet). — [DTF Transfers Now](https://dtftransfersnow.com/products/dtf-heat-transfer)
- Desktop film: A3+ 13 × 19 in (330 × 483 mm) sheets; A3 297 × 420 mm sheets; 13 in × 328 ft (100 m) rolls "for any A3+ printer". — [Walmart A3+ listing (snippet)](https://www.walmart.com/ip/A-SUB-DTF-Film-13x19-DTF-Transfer-Sheets-A3-100-Sheets-Sublimation-Paper-Dark-Fabrics-Clear-PET-DTF-Paper-DIY-Cotton-T-shirt-DTF-Ink-Printer/5120389967); [BCH Technologies A3 (snippet)](https://bchtechnologies.com/products/premium-dtf-direct-to-film-transfer-film-100-sheets-bulk-package-cold-hot-peel-size-a3-11-7-x-16-5-or-297-mm-x-420-mm); [Kingdom DTF 13 in roll (snippet)](https://kingdomdtf.com/products/dtf-film-13-x-328-matte-roll-hot-peel)

**Spacing and margins**
- 1/4–1/2 in between designs; designs at least 0.25–0.5 in from sheet edges. — [Atlanta Vinyl](https://atlantavinylstore.com/blogs/news/dtf-gang-sheets-explained-how-to-maximize-every-inch)
- 1/2 in clear at sheet edges, 1/4 in minimum between designs, more for heavy ink coverage (powder bridging); DTF film has no grain, so designs can be rotated freely. — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/connect/article/dtf-gang-sheet-batch-production-guide)
- "No single universal measurement"; small logos need minimal margin, large prints and irregular shapes more. — [DTFSheet](https://dtfsheet.com/blogs/blog/dtf-gang-sheet-spacing) (16 Mar 2026)
- Ninja: back collar + full back can be one print with ~2–3 in gap. — [Ninja](https://ninjatransfers.com/pages/dtf-transfer-placement-guide)

**File requirements**
- 300 DPI at final print size, transparent background, fully opaque colours, lines ≥ 0.5 mm, do not mirror. — [t-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/)
- PNG preferred; 300 DPI at final size (4 × 4 in = 1200 × 1200 px; 12 × 12 in = 3600 × 3600 px); lines/strokes < 6 px may not print; fonts below 8–10 pt risky; trim canvas to design (1–2 mm bleed max); Canva exports PNG at 96 DPI by default, use "PDF Print" for large prints. — [Transfer Superstars](https://www.transfersuperstars.com/blogs/news/dtf-file-requirements-how-to-prep-your-artwork-for-perfect-prints)
- White in a DTF file prints as white ink; a 300 DPI file scaled 200% becomes 150 DPI effective. — [Gelato](https://www.gelato.com/connect/article/dtf-gang-sheet-batch-production-guide)
- Minimum line thickness conflicts: 0.5 mm (t-shirt.ca; Garment Printing AU), 0.018 in / 5 px at 300 ppi ≈ 0.42 mm (Atlanta Vinyl, snippet), 6 px ≈ 0.51 mm (Transfer Superstars), 2.5 pt / 11 px ≈ 0.9 mm (custom order form, snippet). — [t-shirt.ca](https://t-shirt.ca/dtf-knowledge-hub/dtf-artwork-requirements-and-file-formats/); [Transfer Superstars](https://www.transfersuperstars.com/blogs/news/dtf-file-requirements-how-to-prep-your-artwork-for-perfect-prints); [Garment Printing AU (snippet)](https://garmentprinting.com.au/page/products/gang-sheets-dtf-transfers); [Jotform order form (snippet)](https://form.jotform.com/222516796330053)
- Ninja upload limit: max 50 MB, 300 DPI recommended. — [Ninja](https://ninjatransfers.com/pages/dtf-transfer-placement-guide)

### Inferences
- Pixel sizes at 300 DPI for presets: 22 × 12 in = 6600 × 3600; 22 × 24 = 6600 × 7200; 22 × 36 = 6600 × 10800; 22 × 60 = 6600 × 18000; 22 × 120 = 6600 × 36000; 22 × 240 = 6600 × 72000; A4 = 2480 × 3508; A3 = 3508 × 4961; 13 × 19 in = 3900 × 5700.
- Usable width on a 22 in sheet after 0.5 in edge margins = 21 in, so one row fits e.g. 1 × 12 in back + 2 × 4 in chest logos, or 5 × 3.5 in left-chest logos with 0.25 in gaps.

- A4 DTF film for desktop printers: only a low-quality marketplace listing found.

## 4. Sublimation textile notes

### Takeaway
Sublimation needs high-polyester (≥60–75%, ideally 100%) light-coloured fabric or poly-coated blanks, and the print must be mirrored (unlike DTF). Desktop sheet sizes are A4, A3/11 × 17 in and A3+/13 × 19 in; larger textiles use 24 in+ rolls.

### Cited Findings
- Image must be mirrored before printing (usually default in the print utility); 100% polyester garments press at ~385–400°F (196–204°C) for 45–60 s, medium pressure. — [Heat Press Nation](https://heatpressnation.com/blogs/blog/how-to-sublimate-a-t-shirt)
- Polyester thresholds vary: ≥65% (one guide), ≥75% (library workshop), ≥60% or poly-coated (Denver Public Library). — [Gelato (snippet)](https://www.gelato.com/blog/how-to-sublimate-a-shirt); [Denver Library (snippet)](https://denverlibrary.org/node/697734); [OPPL (snippet)](https://oppl.org/use-your-library/creative-studio-services/sublimation-printer-heat-presses)
- Exception: Siser EasySubli printed without a cutter uses mirror OFF. — [heyletsmakestuff (snippet)](https://heyletsmakestuff.com/wprm_print/using-siser-easysubli-with-a-sawgrass-printer)
- Printful: sublimation works best on light-coloured fabrics that are at least partial polyester blends or polymer-coated surfaces. — [Printful blog](https://www.printful.com/blog/bestselling-sublimation-products)
- Sawgrass SG1000 standard tray takes up to A3 / 11 × 17 in; optional bypass tray enables A3+ / 13 × 19 in (approx. 330 × 483 mm vs A3 297 × 420 mm). — [Longforte / Sawgrass TruePix](https://www.longforte.com/products/sawgrass-truepix-sublimation-paper-13-x-19-a3)
- Sublimation paper commonly sold in A4 (210 × 297) and A3 (297 × 420). — search snippets, e.g. [Pencarrie (snippet)](https://legacy.pencarrie.com/catalogue?p=mt150)
- One sublimation sizing page (unverified, low-quality) suggests max adult design ~10.5 × 12.5 in, start at 8 in wide for adult S. — reported via search; treat as unreliable.

### Inferences (which shirt placements fit A4 for sublimation)
- Fit on A4 (210 × 297 mm): left/right chest, pocket, back neck, short sleeve, centre chest (≤8 in W, or up to ~11 in W rotated if H ≤ 8 in), infant/onesie, toddler, youth XS (8 × 9 tight), cap, mouse pad, hoodie pocket.
- Need A3 / 11 × 17 in: standard adult full front ≤11 in (11.5 in is tight), youth S–XL fronts, upper back 12 × 4 (rotated), long-sleeve vertical ≤14 in, hoodie front 10–11 in, tote 9 × 9.
- Need A3+ 13 × 19 in: full back 12 × 14, 12–12.5 in fronts (2XL–3XL), garden flag 12 × 18, 12 × 18 pillow, long-sleeve 16–18 in.
- Need roll (24 in sublimation roll / 22 in DTF gang sheet): oversize 13 × 15 and above, Printful 15 × 18, square pillows 16–22 in, blankets, pet bandanas.
- Caveat: for sublimation a design wider than the sheet can be split across sheets, but that leaves seams; the above assumes single-sheet transfer.

### Gaps
- Sawgrass and Siser official sublimation shirt-size guides, Cricut heat transfer size charts and Swing Design guides were not retrieved.
- No authoritative source found for sublimation-specific sock, bib, koozie or face mask templates.
