# Example designs

Six example designs shown on the home page ("Bring your own design"). Supplied by the project owner from the
`Sublimation & DTF images/` folder, who confirmed on 2026-10-29 that they may be used in the UI.

Only designs without third-party characters, brand logos, watermarks or people were taken. Images from that folder
showing characters (One Piece, Hello Kitty, Gundam, Tom & Jerry, Dragon Ball, Evangelion, Roblox faces), car brands
(Mercedes, Porsche, Nissan) or real people are deliberately not used.
